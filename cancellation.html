APPOINTMENT POLICY

Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Our cancellation policy helps us manage appointments fairly and allows us to offer cancelled appointments to other clients.

1. Appointment Deposit

A £15 deposit is required to secure every appointment.

The £15 deposit will be deducted from the total cost of your treatment on the day of your appointment.

£15 Deposit

Your deposit is not an additional charge. It is deducted from the final cost of your treatment.

2. Cancelling Your Appointment

If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please provide at least 48 hours' notice.

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled appointment time will not be charged a cancellation fee.

3. Cancellations Within 48 Hours

If an appointment is cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice, the £15 deposit is non-refundable.

The same policy applies where an appointment is cancelled on the day of the appointment or where a client does not attend their appointment.

Less Than 48 Hours

Your £15 deposit will be retained if you cancel with less than 48 hours' notice or do not attend your appointment.

4. Rescheduling

If you need to change your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible.

Where at least 48 hours' notice is provided, we will do our best to accommodate a change of appointment time, subject to availability.

Changes requested with less than 48 hours' notice may result in the £15 deposit being retained.

5. No-Shows

If you do not attend your appointment without contacting us, the £15 deposit will be non-refundable.

Repeated no-shows or late cancellations may affect our ability to accept future bookings.

6. Contact Us

If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible.

Email:
moncherie.beauty@outlook.com

7. Quick Summary

  • A £15 deposit is required to secure every appointment.
  • The £15 deposit is deducted from your total treatment cost.
  • Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the appointment will not incur a cancellation charge.
  • Cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice will result in the £15 deposit being non-refundable.
  • The £15 deposit is also non-refundable in the event of a no-show.

Thank you for respecting our cancellation policy and helping us keep appointments available for all of our clients.
